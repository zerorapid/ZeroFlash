When you visit a website on your phone, you want it to open instantly, react immediately when you tap, and stay still on your screen. Google has three simple rules to measure how pleasant a website feels for real people.

Let's break down these three simple rules and explore how we keep our site fast and clean:

### 1. Fast Loading

This is simply how long it takes for the main part of the page—like the headline or the main photo—to appear on your screen.

*   **How we make it fast**: We display our stories and images instantly without forcing your phone to download heavy, slow files beforehand. This lets your web browser start displaying the page in the blink of an eye.
*   **Font optimization**: We load our typography in a way that shows default text first, so you never have to wait just to start reading the first paragraph.

### 2. Instant Tapping

This measures how quickly the page responds when you tap a button, open a menu, or write a comment. If you tap a button and nothing happens for half a second, the page feels broken or frozen.

*   **How we make it fast**: We keep our web code extremely light and clean. This ensures your phone's processor can handle any tap or click immediately, with absolutely zero delay.

### 3. Stable Text

Have you ever been reading an article on your phone, and just as you were about to tap a link, a slow picture suddenly loaded and pushed the text down? This is frustrating because it makes you tap the wrong thing by mistake.

*   **How we make it fast**: We define the exact sizes of our pictures and advertisement spaces before they load. This reserves the perfect amount of empty space in advance, so everything on the page stays completely still while loading.

### Summary

By keeping our website simple, lightweight, and focused on clean text and pictures, we can make reading articles a breeze for everyone, no matter how slow or fast their internet connection is.
