Today, most of us read articles and browse stories on our phones. Yet, many websites are still designed for big desktop monitors, loading endless columns, annoying pop-ups, and complicated sidebar menus.

**Keeping things simple is more than just making a site look pretty—it makes it work much better.**

When we remove unnecessary visual clutter, we get three major benefits:

1.  **Super-fast loading**: A page with fewer items loads much faster on slow cellular connections or older smartphones.
2.  **Easy, distraction-free reading**: A single, clean column of text naturally guides your eyes. You won't get distracted by blinking ads or complicated side columns.
3.  **No sudden text movement**: When there are no heavy dynamic widgets loading in the background, the text stays completely still, so you never lose your place.

### The Power of One Column

On small phone screens, a single vertical column of text is perfect for scrolling with your thumb. Rather than constantly zooming in or jumping between disconnected boxes on the screen, a simple scrolling page feels totally natural and smooth.

By putting readable letters and comfortable spacing first, reading a news article becomes peaceful and easy to understand.
